More Than a Stay,
It's an Experience

Glenvia was born from a simple dream — to share the untouched beauty of the Eastern Himalayas with travellers seeking something real. Nestled in the heart of mountain towns, our homestays offer the perfect blend of local charm, modern comfort, and breathtaking views.

From the bustling streets of Gangtok to the serene tea gardens of Chalsa, every Glenvia property is hand-picked to give you an authentic Himalayan experience you'll never forget.
